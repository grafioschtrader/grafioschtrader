[historical] Enter the ISIN of the share, e.g. US0378331005; a ticker symbol such as AAPL, a WKN, a CUSIP or a SEDOL are accepted as well. Prefer the ISIN: the ISIN and the ticker of one company can deliver two different price series. Requires a personal API key from Eulerpool, which an administrator stores for this connector. The free plan allows 100,000 requests per month, is limited to private, non-commercial use and expects the note "Data by Eulerpool" wherever the data is shown publicly. Shares only – ETFs, funds and indices are not available from this source. Delivers split-adjusted end-of-day prices. A share of its home market arrives in the currency of that market. A London listing does not: it is quoted neither in pence nor in pounds, so check the first prices of such a share against your own source. Dividends are split-adjusted; amounts of some names (especially older Swiss series) should be checked. [intra] Use the same identifier as for historical prices. The last price is delayed (about 15 minutes on the free plan). It is taken from the listing whose currency matches the instrument.
