Stock Analysis provides shares and ETFs of most major exchanges with the daily history since the listing, without an API key. The prices are adjusted for splits but not for dividends.
The URL extension is the path of the instrument page: for a US listing s/ followed by the symbol, e.g. "s/aapl" for Apple or "s/brk.b" for Berkshire Hathaway B; for any other exchange a/ followed by the exchange code, a dash and the symbol, e.g. "a/SWX-NESN" for Nestlé on SIX, "a/ETR-SAP" for SAP on Xetra or "a/AMS-IWDA" for an ETF on Euronext Amsterdam. Upper and lower case do not matter.
The search for a security at the data providers derives this extension from the ticker symbol and the exchange. German regional exchanges such as Munich, Stuttgart or Hamburg are not offered.
