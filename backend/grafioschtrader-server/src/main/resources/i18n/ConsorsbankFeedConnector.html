Consorsbank provides current quotes and historical daily prices for securities, forex and available cryptocurrency pairs. No login or API key is required.

Search for the instrument on Consorsbank and select the intended trading venue and currency. In the browser developer tools' network requests, find field=PriceV2 and copy id and rtExchangeCode. For both intraday and history, enter _notation-ID,exchange-code. The search for a security at the data providers finds this value by itself for the venues GT knows; US shares are quoted as Nasdaq Basic (NSB).

  • adidas, Xetra, EUR: _39471840,GER
  • SPDR S&P U.S. Energy Select Sector, SIX, USD: _139582518,SWX
  • EUR/USD, FactSet: _1390634,@W$
  • EUR/CHF, FactSet: _1619898,@W$
  • BTC/USD and BTC/EUR, Bitstamp: _213086262,BTC and _213086260,BTC
  • ETH/USD and ETH/EUR, Bitstamp: _213086261,BTC and _213086259,BTC

The ID identifies a specific listing. General search results may point to inaccessible listings, as observed for Ethereum. Select the trading venue explicitly in that case. Additional forex and crypto pairs require an accessible Consorsbank listing; inverse pairs and cross rates are not calculated.

History is downloaded page by page using HistoryV1 and restricted to the requested date range. It includes closing prices and, where supplied, open, high, low and volume. Forex and crypto weekend observations are retained. Current quotes use the provider's timestamp; delays depend on the venue (typically up to 15 minutes).

Quality limitations: Historical coverage depends on the listing and can contain gaps, especially for bonds. Missing sessions are not filled and no additional dividend or corporate-action adjustments are applied. Adjustments may differ from other providers. Older series can contain erroneous repeated closing prices, as observed for the IEF ETF in 2017/2018. Chart history supplies the same closing prices and is not an independent source for validation.
